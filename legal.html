    Section 03

    Vote Credits & Payments

    No Refunds Policy

    All vote credit purchases are completely final and non-refundable under any circumstances. Credits hold no monetary value outside of this contest platform.

    • Vote credits can only be used to vote within the CG Baby of the Year contest.
    • Credits expire at the end of the contest and cannot be carried over.
    • Minimum purchase is 500 credits for ₦500. Credits are priced at ₦1 per credit.
    • All payments are processed securely through Flutterwave via instant bank transfer.
    • CGPIXELS is not responsible for failed bank transfers due to incorrect account details entered by the user.
    • Credits are added automatically upon payment confirmation. If credits are not received within 30 minutes of a successful transfer, contact us via WhatsApp.
    Section 04

    Voting Rules

    • 1 credit = 1 vote. There is no limit on how many credits you can purchase.
    • Votes cast are final and cannot be reversed or refunded.
    • Any attempt to manipulate votes through technical means will result in immediate disqualification.
    • CGPIXELS reserves the right to audit and remove fraudulent votes at any time.
    Section 05

    Contest Phases

    1
    Nominations
    Submit your child's details and photo for admin review. Must be a CGPIXELS photoshoot.
    2
    Free Voting
    All registered users receive free credits to vote. Community rallying determines top nominees.
    3
    Grand Finals
    Top 5 nominees advance. Additional credits can be purchased to vote for your favourite.
    4
    Winner Announcement
    Highest votes at the deadline wins. Winner receives premium shoot package and official recognition.

    All Sales Are Final — No Refunds

    All vote credit purchases made on the CG Baby of the Year platform are completely final and non-refundable under any circumstances.

    Section 01

    Why No Refunds

    Vote credits are a digital contest currency with no monetary value outside this platform. Once purchased and added to your account, they can be immediately used to vote. Due to the nature of digital goods and contest integrity, we are unable to process refunds.

    Section 02

    What Credits Are

    • Digital tokens used exclusively within the CG Baby of the Year contest.
    • They hold no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash.
    • Credits expire at the end of the contest period with no compensation for unused credits.
    • Cannot be transferred between accounts.
    Section 03

    Payment Failures

    If you made a bank transfer and credits were not added within 30 minutes:

    • Confirm the transfer was successful in your bank app.
    • Ensure you transferred the exact amount to the correct account number provided.
    • Contact us via WhatsApp with your transaction reference number immediately.
    • We will investigate and credit your account if payment is confirmed on our end.
    The Only Exception

    If you were charged but credits were never added due to a verified technical error on our end, we will either add the credits or issue a refund after investigation. Contact us with proof of payment.

    Section 04

    Duplicate Payments

    If you accidentally made the same payment twice, contact us immediately with both transaction references. We will investigate and refund the duplicate payment within 7 business days.

    Section 05

    Chargebacks

    Important

    Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute for a valid credit purchase will result in immediate suspension of your account and disqualification from the contest.
